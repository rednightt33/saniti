Generated by `scripts/generate_feature_07_catalog.py` (EXEC-RADAR). The same text is in `Feature_Catalog` v1 and `Column_Catalog`. Filters and their wording: `verification/radar/RADAR_PAGE_SPEC.md`; definitions and worked examples: `SCREENER_LIBRARY.md`.

Sessions: ticker sessions as Feature 06: the dates on which the ticker traded on any board; a session without a Regular trade counts zero on the Regular board. A window is NULL until it is full; a ratio with a zero denominator is NULL. Own-history ranks: own-history rank: 100 x (previous values strictly below today's) / 252 over the ticker's 252 previous sessions; NULL unless 252 complete values (EXEC-RADAR D1). Cross-stock ranks: 100 x (other stocks of the population strictly below) / (population - 1); NULL for a population of one.

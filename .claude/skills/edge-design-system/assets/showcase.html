KPI tiles

Label (with its timeframe), one-line purpose, value at 18px/600, change line, 64×26 sparkline (up when the last value is at or above the first). A tile for broker data carries the amber date pill.

Treemap colours

Seven steps of the 1-day change: ≥3%, ≥1.5%, ≥0.5% green; within ±0.5% grey; then the red steps. Text is white on the two darker steps.

Focus, contrast, touch (decisions 12-14, 2026-11-27)

Click a control: no ring. Press Tab: one grey 2px ring (--focus-ring), never blue; a field shows its selected state only, a select opened with the mouse too. Grey labels (--text-3) and the tag ink on its tint read 4.5:1 or more. On a touch screen the small controls take a 44 × 44 px area to tap, with no change of layout.
